StrumDaily Privacy Policy

Version 1.0 · Effective 10 November 2026

StrumDaily is a daily practice tracker for guitar and bass players. This policy explains what personal data is processed when you use the app, why, on what legal basis, who receives it, how long it is kept and what your rights are. In short: your practice log stays on your iPhone unless you turn on cloud backup; usage and crash data is sent only if you allow it; we never sell your data, show ads or track you across other apps.

1. Who is responsible

The data controller is Sergen Tatlı, an individual developer established in Türkiye. Contact for anything about your data: sergentatl@gmail.com. We have not appointed a data protection officer; we are not required to.

2. Data that stays on your iPhone

Your practice log (days, minutes, techniques), streak, badges, the name and weekly goal you choose, reminders and settings are stored only on your device. We cannot see them. Deleting the app deletes them.

Microphone. The tuner listens to your instrument to detect its pitch. Sound is analysed on your iPhone in real time and is never recorded, stored or sent anywhere. The microphone is used only while the tuner is open, and only after you allow it in iOS.

Reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device. No server is involved.

We do not collect your name from Apple, your contacts, location, photos, health data, advertising identifier or any special category of personal data. Crash reports and usage events never contain your name, email, practice notes or IP address.

You can withdraw consent at any time in Profile › Settings › Privacy. Sending stops immediately; withdrawal does not affect processing done before.

4. Who receives your data

We use these service providers (processors), bound by data processing agreements:

  • Supabase Inc. (USA): database and sign-in for cloud backup.
  • RevenueCat Inc. (USA): purchase and subscription management.
  • Apple Inc. (USA / Ireland): payment, App Store and Sign in with Apple. Apple processes payments as an independent controller under its own privacy policy; we never see your card details.
  • Functional Software Inc. (Sentry) (USA): crash reports, only with your consent.
  • PostHog Inc. (servers in the European Union): usage analytics, only with your consent.

We do not sell, rent or "share" (as defined by California law) your personal data, and we do not use it for advertising. We may disclose data if required by law or a court order.

5. International transfers

Our providers process data outside Türkiye and outside the European Economic Area, including in the United States. For transfers from the EEA and the UK we rely on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses (and the UK Addendum) or the EU-US Data Privacy Framework where the provider is certified. For transfers from Türkiye under KVKK Article 9 we rely on the explicit consent you give in the app: for cloud backup before you sign in, and for crash and usage data when you allow sharing. Purchase records reach RevenueCat because that is necessary to provide the purchase you make (KVKK Art. 9(6)(b)). Without these, nothing leaves your iPhone except what Apple itself processes for the App Store.

6. Your rights

You can ask to access, correct, delete or receive a copy (portability) of your personal data, to restrict or object to its processing, and to withdraw consent. Residents of Türkiye have the rights in KVKK Article 11 (see the Turkish version). Write to sergentatl@gmail.com; we answer free of charge within 30 days. We may ask you to confirm your identity, for example by writing from the email address of your account.

You can delete your account and all cloud data yourself in Profile › Cloud backup › Delete account. If you used Sign in with Apple, the app also revokes its access to your Apple ID.

You have the right to complain to a data protection authority: in Türkiye the Personal Data Protection Authority (kvkk.gov.tr), in the EU the authority of your country, in the UK the ICO.

7. California residents

In the last 12 months we collected, depending on the features you used: identifiers (anonymous Apple user ID, account ID, random installation ID), commercial information (purchase records), internet or other electronic activity (anonymous usage events, only with consent) and customer records (practice data you back up). We collect them for the purposes in section 3. We do not sell or share personal information, do not use sensitive personal information, and will not discriminate against you for exercising your rights to know, correct and delete, which you can use by writing to us.

8. Security

Data in transit is encrypted (HTTPS). Cloud backup data is protected by row-level security so that each account can only reach its own rows. Sign-in tokens are kept in the iOS Keychain.

9. Children

StrumDaily is not directed at children under 13 (or under 16 in the EU where national law requires parental consent). We do not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child has given us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Automated decisions

We make no decisions based solely on automated processing that have legal or similarly significant effects on you.

11. Changes

If this policy changes we will update the version and date above, and for material changes tell you in the app and, where required, ask for your consent again.
